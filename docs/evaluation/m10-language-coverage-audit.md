The Appendix 3 equivalence is not a formula from TOEIC 840 to the applicant's final English points.
For Information Engineering, p.52 establishes that English is worth 100 maximum points and is used
with the specialist examination and oral examination. It does not publish the mapping from a TOEIC
score to earned points, a cutoff, or an admission result. Those values must not be inferred by
combining unrelated rules.

## Not found in the reviewed source

The current 334-Fact KB contains zero matches for `JLPT`, `日本語能力試験`, `J.TEST`, or `J-TEST`.
The reviewed plan consequently contains no rule that requires, accepts, rejects, or substitutes
either JLPT or J.TEST. Product output must say only that the current reviewed material contains no
clear basis; it must not say that either exam is definitely unnecessary or rejected.
